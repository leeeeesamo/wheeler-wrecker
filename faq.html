Frequently asked questions

Answers about towing, recovery, our locations and how to get service.

Is Wheeler available 24 hours?

Yes. Towing and recovery dispatch is available 24 hours a day. The office hours for both Bessemer and Alabaster are Monday–Friday 8 AM–5 PM and Saturday 8 AM–12 PM.

What types of vehicles does Wheeler tow?

Wheeler offers light-, medium- and heavy-duty towing, including everyday cars, classic cars, pickups, commercial trucks and buses.

Do you offer heavy-duty rotator services?

Yes. Wheeler lists rotator recovery and rotator/crane services among its capabilities. Call dispatch with your location, vehicle type and recovery needs.

Where does Wheeler operate?

Wheeler serves Bessemer, Birmingham, Alabaster, Helena, Pelham, Calera, McCalla and surrounding Jefferson and Shelby County communities, with service available across Alabama. Call to confirm availability for your exact location and services outside the state.

Can I submit an urgent tow request through the website?

No. Urgent towing and recovery requests must be made by phone. Forms and email are only for non-urgent inquiries.

Does Wheeler work with commercial fleets?

Yes. Wheeler welcomes inquiries from trucking companies, fleet operators, equipment owners and other businesses seeking towing and recovery support.

How can I retrieve a towed vehicle?

Call the Wheeler shop handling the tow first to verify the vehicle location, required release arrangements, applicable fees and office hours. Do not visit without confirming details.
