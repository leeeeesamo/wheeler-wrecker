Wheeler Wrecker Service
Staff dashboard — PRE-LAUNCH PREVIEW
This dashboard is a visual stub and is NOT functional. It has no backend, no authentication, and stores nothing. Before launch it will be wired to Supabase Auth + Postgres (see docs/backend-and-admin-plan.md). Do not deploy as-is. Access will be password-protected and restricted to Wheeler staff once the backend is live.

1 · Sign in

Wheeler staff will sign in with an email + password managed by Supabase Auth. Only approved admin accounts can see this dashboard.

2 · Customer reviews

Moderation queue for reviews submitted through the website. Approved reviews may appear on the Reviews page. Nothing is published automatically.

No reviews yet — backend not connected.
Schema: reviews(id, author, rating, body, source, status, created_at)

3 · Private feedback

Non-public customer feedback and questions submitted from the review/feedback forms. Staff can reply by phone or email.

No feedback yet — backend not connected.
Schema: feedback(id, name, contact, message, created_at)

4 · Promotions

Configure the customer appreciation offer here — no code edits needed. Terms must be approved by Wheeler first (see docs/discount-program.md). Discounts are never tied to leaving a review.

5 · Redemptions

Log of discount uses. Helps Wheeler track the promotion without requiring reviews or proof of posting.

No redemptions — backend not connected.
Schema: redemptions(id, promo_id, customer_ref, used_at)
