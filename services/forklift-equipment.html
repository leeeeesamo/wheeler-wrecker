Forklifts and equipment, moved with care.

Need machinery unloaded, cargo transferred between trailers, or equipment moved into position? Wheeler Wrecker Service can bring the right forklift and an operator to your shop, yard, or job site, with options ranging from light-duty to heavy-duty forklifts. Call us with the details of your job for a quote.

  • Forklift relocation between sites and facilities
  • Equipment transport support for contractors and businesses
  • Two dispatch locations: Bessemer and Alabaster

When you call, have ready the equipment type and approximate size or weight, pickup and drop-off locations, site access notes and your preferred timing. For urgent recovery needs, always call dispatch directly.
