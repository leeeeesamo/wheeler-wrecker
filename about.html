Our story

Built on experience and service.

Wheeler Wrecker Service has served Alabama communities since 1967 and continues through three generations of family ownership. With more than 30 trucks, our team handles everything from passenger cars and classic vehicles to heavy-duty commercial towing, rotator recovery and equipment transportation.

We operate from Bessemer and Alabaster and support customers across Jefferson and Shelby counties and throughout Alabama.

Our promise: “Large or small, we handle ’em all.”
