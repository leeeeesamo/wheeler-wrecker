# Wheeler Customer Appreciation Discount — Pending Terms

**Status: NOT ACTIVE. Do not publish or redeem until Wheeler approves terms in writing.**

## Compliance constraints (non-negotiable)

- A discount must NEVER be offered in exchange for a Google, Facebook, or any public review.
- No review screenshot, rating, or positive sentiment is required or checked.
- Review links and discount access are completely independent features.
- If a private satisfaction survey is ever used, its terms and any incentive language
  must be reviewed for FTC/Google compliance before launch.
